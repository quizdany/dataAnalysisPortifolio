# Rwanda energy analysis

Five World Bank energy indicators for Rwanda, 1990–2020 (electricity access, renewable consumption, renewable output, CO2 emissions, energy intensity).

- cleaning and aligning the series by year
- rolling averages and growth rates
- 5-year forecasts with ARIMA, exponential smoothing and Prophet
- regression: renewables explain only 11.5% of the variation in CO2 (R² = 0.115)
- Dash dashboard to compare indicators

Notebook: `RwandaEnergyProject.ipynb` (CSVs are in this folder).
